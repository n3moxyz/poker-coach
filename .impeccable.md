# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Poker beginners learning Texas Hold'em. They come to the app curious but potentially intimidated by poker's complexity. Their job: build real poker skills through practice, not just memorize rules. They use the app in focused learning sessions (quizzes, modules) and casual play sessions (vs AI). They want to feel progress and understand *why* plays are good or bad, not just be told.

### Brand Personality
**Sharp. Sleek. Competitive.**

The voice is confident and knowledgeable — like a sharp-dressed poker coach who respects your intelligence. Not condescending, not overly casual. The tone says "you're here to get better, and I'll make sure you do." Think precision over playfulness, but never cold.

### Emotional Goals
- **"I'm in control"** — Every concept clicks, progress is visible, decisions feel informed
- **"This is actually fun"** — Learning feels like playing, not studying. Gamification serves engagement, not just retention

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Casino dark theme — deep blacks, felt greens, gold accents. Premium and immersive, not garish or flashy
- **References**: Brilliant.org (elegant interactive learning, builds understanding step-by-step), Chess.com (clean game UI, progressive difficulty, polished competitive feel)
- **Anti-references**: Flashy casino apps with neon overload; overly cute/childish gamification; cluttered dashboards with too many competing elements
- **Theme**: Dark mode only. The casino atmosphere IS the brand — rich blacks (#0a0e12), felt green (#0d3320), gold (#f5c518)
- **Typography**: DM Sans (clean, modern body), Playfair Display (premium branding moments), JetBrains Mono (cards, data)

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — Every element earns its place. If it doesn't help the user understand or decide, remove it. Inspired by Brilliant's focused learning layouts.

2. **Progress is always visible** — XP, streaks, module mastery, hand grades — the user should never wonder "am I getting better?" Metrics are prominent but not overwhelming.

3. **Premium restraint** — Gold accents, glow effects, and animations are used sparingly for emphasis, not everywhere for flash. A gold shimmer on a mastered module means something because it's rare.

4. **Teach through interaction** — Prefer interactive elements (range matrices, equity bars, hand replays) over walls of text. The UI itself should build poker intuition.

5. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AAA compliance target. High contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, screen reader support, color-blind-safe feedback indicators. Never rely on color alone to convey meaning (card suits, grades, achievement tiers).

### Animation Philosophy
- Animations serve feedback, not decoration
- Entrance animations: subtle slide-up + fade (0.3-0.4s)
- Hover effects: lift + border glow (cards), scale 0.98 (buttons)
- Celebration moments: XP pop, gold glow pulse — earned, not constant
- Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (out-expo) for most transitions
